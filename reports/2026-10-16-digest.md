## Content ideas for social media

1. **Is SEO Dead? Meet AEO** (Carousel) - Essential for marketers looking to stay ahead of the shift toward AI-powered search engines.
   - Slide 1: Traditional SEO focuses on keywords and rankings, but AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity are changing how users find recommendations.
   - Slide 2: Rule #1 of Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is to answer the question first before diving into lengthy details.
   - Slide 3: Use the exact conversational language and search questions your customers use daily in comments and searches.
   - Slide 4: Keep your content straightforward with clear headings and short paragraphs so both humans and machines can understand it easily.
   - Slide 5: Inject human experience, original insights, and case studies to differentiate your content from generic AI information.
   - Slide 6: Audit your current brand visibility by asking AI direct questions like 'Who are the best service providers in the UK?' to see if you appear.
2. **YouTube Just Shifted the Shorts Algorithm** (Short video) - Timely platform update that directly impacts every creator relying on YouTube Shorts for reach.
   - Hook: YouTube completely changed the Shorts algorithm, and it's bad news if you re-upload clips without editing.
   - Point 1: YouTube's October 1 update actively favors original content and reduces reach for channels re-uploading other creators' videos.
   - Point 2: Simple voiceovers describing on-screen action or minor technical edits are no longer enough to count as original work.
   - Point 3: To keep your reach, you need genuine original commentary, unique edits, strong storytelling, or a distinct point of view.
   - Point 4: If you pivot from re-uploads to original work, YouTube will re-evaluate and give your channel a fresh chance to grow.
   - Closing CTA: Are you changing your content strategy after this update? Let us know in the comments.
3. **Why Is Your YouTube Subscriber Growth Dropping?** (Thread) - Addresses a painful macro trend affecting 70% of creators right now.
   - Post 1: Notice your YouTube subscriber growth slowing down lately? You aren't imagining things—new data shows it's happening across the board.
   - Post 2: According to vidIQ data from October 2026, 7 in 10 channels saw their subscriber gain rate fall over the past six months.
   - Post 3: Across 3 million eligible channels, the typical subscriber rate per 1,000 views dropped from 2.2 down to 1.9.
   - Post 4: Interestingly, total views have held steady, meaning the friction is specifically happening at the conversion point from viewer to subscriber.
   - Post 5: If you want to beat this trend, focus heavily on packaging, viewer intent, and clearly demonstrating your unique expertise early in your videos.
4. **PPC & Merchant Center Platform Updates** (Infographic) - Keeps paid media professionals instantly updated on critical workflow and feature adjustments.
   - Section 1: Google Ads has introduced new channel prioritization and budget allocation tools to give advertisers better campaign control.
   - Section 2: Google Merchant Center rolled out a brand new store address attribute for local and product optimization.
   - Section 3: Watch out for current technical glitches, as Merchant Center is facing ongoing video link processing issues.
   - Section 4: Microsoft Ads updated its trend charts by adding helpful OR filters and custom columns for deeper data analysis.
5. **How Public Health Crises Shape Media Cycles** (Newsletter) - Explores how high-stakes global news coverage interacts with digital storytelling and public transparency.
   - Point 1: Major news events like Pope Leo XIV's recent lung surgery at Rome's Gemelli Hospital capture global attention through human vulnerability.
   - Point 2: The 71-year-old Pope's early-stage adenocarcinoma diagnosis was handled with unusual transparency by Vatican officials.
   - Point 3: Because normal lab tests and recovery updates were shared openly, creators were able to build factual health explainers instantly.
   - Point 4: High-emotion global events prove that transparent institutional communication minimizes misinformation and drives engaged public discussion.
6. **Geopolitics, Energy Crises, and Digital Discourse** (Poll) - Taps into ongoing public debates about international policy and public infrastructure vulnerability.

   - Answer Option A: Yes, diplomatic channels will eventually stabilize regional energy grids.
   - Answer Option B: No, recent strikes on Kyiv power infrastructure show ceasefires are fragile.
   - Answer Option C: Unsure, waiting to see how global markets and domestic fuel prices react.
