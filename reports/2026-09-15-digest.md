## Content ideas for social media

1. **"The 20% Budget Leak"**
   * **Format:** Carousel
   * **Angle:** Timely due to new click fraud stats showing 1 in 5 ad clicks never convert—break down how to audit and plug the leaks in paid campaigns.

2. **"Are AI Warnings Overblown or Underestimated?"**
   * **Format:** Poll / Discussion post
   * **Angle:** Capitalizes on recent high-profile pushback against AI narratives to gauge sentiment from industry peers on real vs. overhyped tech risks.

3. **"Stop Burning Ad Spend on Ghost Clicks"**
   * **Format:** Short video (Reels/TikTok)
   * **Angle:** A quick, punchy walkthrough of click fraud signs and practical steps advertisers can take today to protect their PPC ROI.

4. **"The State of AI in Marketing: Fact vs. Fiction"**
   * **Format:** Thread (X/LinkedIn)
   * **Angle:** Filters out the political and cultural noise surrounding AI to focus strictly on what marketers should actually be paying attention to right now.

5. **"3 PPC Metrics You're Ignoring That Are Costing You Money"**
   * **Format:** Single-image infographic
   * **Angle:** Piggybacks on the conversation around wasted ad spend by highlighting overlooked indicators of low-intent or fraudulent traffic.

6. **"Hot Take: Is AI Regulation Moving Too Fast or Too Slow?"**
   * **Format:** Text post with a strong hook
   * **Angle:** Hooks readers by leaning into the current media discourse on AI skepticism and invites comments on how policy shifts impact marketing tech.
