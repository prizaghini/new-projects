## Content ideas for social media

1. **"The 20% Rule" Budget Leak**
   * *Format:* Carousel
   * *Angle:* Break down the stat that 1 in 5 ad clicks never convert and give 3 ways to plug the leak.

2. **Is AI Safety a Hoax or a Real Threat?**
   * *Format:* Poll / LinkedIn Post
   * *Angle:* Capitalize on the mainstream AI debate to ask your audience where they stand on AI regulation vs. rapid innovation.

3. **Stop Paying for Ghost Clicks**
   * *Format:* Short video (Reels/TikTok)
   * *Angle:* A quick, punchy walkthrough of how click fraud sneaks into PPC campaigns and how to audit your traffic sources today.

4. **Hot Take Thread: AI in Marketing**
   * *Format:* X (Twitter) / LinkedIn Thread
   * *Angle:* Separate the AI hype from reality—addressing the latest public skepticism head-on and what it means for marketers using AI tools.

5. **3 PPC Metrics You’re Ignoring (That Are Costing You Money)**
   * *Format:* Infographic / Single Image
   * *Angle:* Shift the focus away from vanity metrics to conversion-intent data in light of high non-conversion rates.

6. **The Future of Ad Spend: Quality Over Quantity**
   * *Format:* Newsletter / Long-form Post
   * *Angle:* Discuss how rising click waste forces marketers to pivot from broad reach to hyper-targeted, high-intent strategies.
