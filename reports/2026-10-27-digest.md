## Content ideas for social media

1. **Why McDonald's Ditched FIFA After 32 Years to Bet on Grimace** (Carousel) - McDonald's historic split from FIFA illustrates how mega-brands are shifting budgets from costly third-party sponsorships into proprietary IP.
   - Slide 1: End of an era: McDonald's officially walks away from its 32-year FIFA World Cup partnership.
   - Slide 2: The catalyst: CFO Ian Borden confirmed that despite high buzz, their largest 2026 World Cup push failed to deliver expected targets.
   - Slide 3: The pivot: Rather than renting sports audiences, McDonald's is redirecting spend directly into owned IP like Grimace and Hamburglar.
   - Slide 4: The takeaway for marketers: Rented cultural authority is getting more expensive, while owned characters and brand assets deliver compounded equity.
2. **Your Website Copy No Longer Wins the Sale in the AI Era** (Thread) - Generative AI engines compile procurement shortlists long before buyers ever visit a vendor site.
   - 1/ Most brands still treat discovery as the moment a buyer visits their website. In the era of AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT, you've already won or lost before that click.
   - 2/ AI search engines do not rely solely on your own landing pages. They evaluate external signals: third-party reviews, press coverage, executive LinkedIn posts, and industry citations.
   - 3/ If an enterprise buyer asks an AI assistant for the best solution, the model builds a shortlist using web-wide consensus, not your polished home page copy.
   - 4/ The solution: Prioritize off-site authority and narrow positioning. AI rewards brands known for a clear, specific discipline rather than broad generalists.
   - 5/ Audit your digital footprint outside your own domain: What is the internet saying about you when your website isn't in the room?
3. **X Algorithm Shift: Reading Time Over Likes** (Poll) - Social platforms are moving away from vanity interactions to reward true dwell time and reading depth.
   - With X shifting algorithmic weight to reading time over likes, how will your content strategy adapt?
   - Double down on in-depth text threads and analyses
   - Keep posts punchy and skim-friendly
   - Add visual hooks to retain attention longer
   - Pivot focus entirely to other platforms
4. **Morrisons Proves You Don't Need New Products for Halloween** (Carousel) - Morrisons and Leo UK demonstrated how everyday supermarket items can hijack cultural moments with zero seasonal product development.
   - Slide 1: Supermarket Halloween marketing usually means aisles of novelty candy and disposable costumes. Morrisons chose a radically different path.
   - Slide 2: In their 'Market Street Monsters' OOH campaign, avocados became 'Monster Eyes', carrots became 'Zombie Fingers', and a 70p red pepper turned into 'Screams'.
   - Slide 3: The creative vehicle: High-contrast still-life photography framed solely by Morrisons' iconic brand green and bold yellow price tags.
   - Slide 4: Why it works: The monster visual arrests the eye, while the immediate low price point gives shoppers an actionable reason to purchase everyday groceries.
   - Slide 5: The playbook: You don't always need new inventory to participate in a holiday—you just need fresh perspective on your existing core catalog.
5. **Google Search Universal Cart: The Zero-Click Storefront Is Here** (Short video) - Google's Universal Cart going live changes how brands must manage search visibility and checkout attribution.
   - Hook: You may never get a website visitor from Google Shopping again—and that might actually be fine.
   - Explain that Google has officially turned on its Universal Cart feature directly inside search results.
   - Break down what this means: Searchers can now add items and complete checkouts natively without landing on your e-commerce domain.
   - Highlight that product feed health and Google Merchant Center compliance now dictate your entire conversion path.
   - Closing CTA: Share this with your paid search team and tell us: will you embrace instant search-checkout or do you fear losing customer site visits?
6. **Stop Treating B2B Attribution Like an Exact Science** (Newsletter) - With nonlinear buyer journeys breaking traditional tracking, B2B leaders must treat attribution as a compass rather than a definitive map.
   - Context: 80% of marketers struggle with comprehensive planning, while multi-touch buyer paths make flawless attribution impossible.
   - The shift: Instead of obsessing over which single touchpoint generated an MQL, mature marketing teams are tracking aggregated directional signals.
   - What to measure: Evaluate aggregate momentum—branded search volume, account-level engagement spikes, and qualitative pipeline velocity.
   - Actionable advice: Frame marketing results to executive leadership as a guiding compass showing commercial trajectory, not an audited receipt of isolated attribution.
7. **Inside Accenture Song's $1B Bet on Agentic Commerce** (Infographic) - Agency business models are being rapidly redesigned around autonomous agent shopping and creator distribution.
   - Section 1: Leadership Transition — Ndidi Oteh's growth playbook one year after taking over Accenture Song.
   - Section 2: The Core Asset — Integration of creator agency Whalar to capture creator-led consumer distribution.
   - Section 3: The Financial Milestone — Rapidly scaling an active $1 billion new-business pipeline around agentic commerce solutions.
   - Section 4: The Strategic Takeaway — How marketing services firms are pivoting from traditional agency retainers to automated AI agent infrastructure.
