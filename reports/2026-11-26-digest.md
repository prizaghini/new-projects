## Content ideas for social media

1. **Stop asking if you're 'falling behind' on AI** (Thread) - Shift the narrative from fear of missing out to strategic business application.
   - The question 'Am I falling behind on AI?' is a trap.
   - Consultant Vaclav Vincalek notes that most businesses aren't behind on tech, they are behind on strategy.
   - AI is an accelerator; it simply makes existing gaps and strengths more visible.
   - Winning companies don't use AI to invent an edge; they use it to widen an existing one.
   - Stop chasing tools and start auditing your business strategy today.
2. **6 lessons to build an internal AI culture** (Carousel) - Actionable advice for internal adoption based on the successful PokerStars model.
   - Lesson 1: Invite, don't force. Create an opt-in network for AI enthusiasts.
   - Lesson 2: Let peers teach peers; they often have the best context for solving each other's problems.
   - Lesson 3: Focus workshops on 'safe use' first, then spend time building tools.
   - Lesson 4: Log and value everything. Track time saved to prove ROI to leadership.
   - Lesson 5: Protect learning time. Give employees dedicated hours in the diary to master specific tools.
3. **Is your business truly 'Tech-Ready'?** (Poll) - Engage followers on the friction points caused by poor tool integration.
   - What is the biggest blocker to your team's efficiency right now?
   - Missed deadlines and poor collaboration
   - Tools that don't play well together
   - High operational costs
   - Lack of clear strategy for new tech
4. **3 Microsoft Ads updates to watch** (Infographic) - Quick summary of recent PPC platform changes for time-pressed marketers.
   - Microsoft Ads has introduced URL inclusions to AI Max for Search.
   - A new AI-powered Discover content feed is now live.
   - These changes represent a push toward more automated, AI-driven ad placements.
   - Audit your current campaigns to see how these placements fit your goals.
   - Stay tuned for more performance updates regarding the November Demand Gen drops.
5. **The 2027 Economic Outlook: What Marketers Need to Know** (Newsletter) - Translating macroeconomic data into marketing planning context.
   - KPMG's latest outlook sees 2026 eurozone growth at 1.0%.
   - Forecast for 2027 is slightly better at 1.2%.
   - Rising inflation and job insecurity are weighing on consumer spending.
   - Plan your 2027 budgets with a focus on efficiency and proven channels.
   - Be mindful that economic pressure changes buying behavior; prioritize retention.
6. **How to build an AI internal network (without a mandate)** (Short video) - Sharing the success of PokerStars' bottom-up approach to tech adoption.
   - Hook: Trying to get your team to adopt AI but hitting a wall?
   - Explain that mandating tools rarely works; creating a community does.
   - Highlight the PokerStars approach: 160+ members built over 120 assistants voluntarily.
   - Mention the importance of peer-to-peer teaching drop-ins.
   - Closing CTA: Are you building an internal AI community or just buying licenses?
