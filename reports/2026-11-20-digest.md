## Content ideas for social media

1. **Why 89% of AI Agents Are Stuck in 'Pilot' Mode** (Thread) - Addresses the reality gap between corporate hype and operational reality.
   - Did you know 71% of companies use AI agents, but only 11% have them in production?
   - The biggest bottleneck isn't the AI tech itself; it's the lack of proper orchestration.
   - Nearly 50% of these agents currently exist in disconnected silos.
   - Stop asking 'Build vs. Buy' and start asking 'Can we govern this?'
   - Successful deployment requires controls that match the specific risk of the business process.
2. **The 'GTA Flu' Marketing Lesson** (Carousel) - Uses a major cultural event to explain the power of reactive social strategy.
   - The 'GTA 6 Flu' is real: workforce attendance dropped as the game launched worldwide.
   - Even if your brand isn't gaming-related, pop-culture moments are 'singularity' events.
   - Relatability wins: focus on the shared human experience (e.g., PTO excuses or spoiler anxiety).
   - Hardware demand spike: PS5 search interest is surging alongside launch day hype.
   - Your lesson: Don't ignore massive cultural waves; build content that taps into the conversation.
3. **Is Your YouTube Studio Layout Changing?** (Short video) - Helps creators navigate a confusing platform interface update.
   - Hook: Can't find your channel stats? YouTube just moved your dashboard.
   - Explain that 'Analytics' is being renamed or moved to the new 'Insights' tab.
   - Address the confusion over Shorts views: they now split into 'Starts/replays' vs 'Engaged views'.
   - Explain that 'Engaged views' exclude loops, giving you a better look at retention.
   - CTA: Check your Studio today and tell me if the new layout makes your data clearer.
4. **The Future of Shopping: Marketing to AI Agents** (Newsletter) - Provides a high-level view of where marketing strategy is shifting in 2027.
   - The shift from marketing to people to marketing to AI agents.
   - What happens when your customer isn't a human, but an algorithm making the buying decision?
   - Walmart's latest strategy: prioritizing product discovery even as their ad growth slows.
   - Why storytelling remains the key differentiator even in agentic commerce.
   - Gartner's 2027 prediction: The challenge is now managing the consequences of operating at 'AI scale'.
5. **PPC Update Alert: New Google Ads Testing** (Thread) - Crucial for PPC managers needing to optimize campaigns for new Google UI/UX tests.
   - Google is testing 'blue' product names in Shopping Ads in AI mode.
   - Watch out for new ad formats appearing in AI mode.
   - Big changes to campaign-level display targeting are hitting Demand Gen.
   - Don't forget: Google now requires reconfirmation for EU political ads status.
   - Stay ahead by monitoring how these UI changes affect your CTR next week.
6. **Is Your Creator Payout threshold dropping?** (Poll) - Engages creators on platform monetization trends.
   - Poll: Does a $20 payout threshold (down from $100) change how you view YouTube monetization?
   - Yes, I'll reach my payout goal much faster.

   - I need more info on how this test works.
