## Content ideas for social media

1. **Why People Post About Products, but Engage With People** (Carousel) - Explaining the massive engagement gap between corporate product content and human-focused culture content on LinkedIn.
   - Hook: Your team is your best marketing asset.
   - A 2026 analysis of 200 LinkedIn company pages showed an 82% engagement boost for culture posts over product posts.
   - People inherently trust other people more than a brand logo.
   - Start featuring your team's real stories rather than just product features.
   - CTA: How are you highlighting your team culture this month?
2. **Is Your Brand Ready for the New AI Age Check?** (Thread) - Discussing the strategic impact of Instagram's global expansion of AI age detection for brands reaching Gen Z audiences.
   - Instagram is rolling out global AI-powered age detection for teen accounts.
   - The system scans post captions, images, and user activity.
   - Marketers can no longer rely purely on self-reported creator audience demographics.
   - Strategy shift: Review your current campaign targeting for younger demographics.
   - Don't let your ads get hidden by automatic protective settings.
3. **The 'Tech Titan' Downfall Lesson** (Short video) - Using the high-profile departure of Nvidia's CEO to discuss business stability and AI infrastructure investments.
   - Hook: Can a CEO’s resignation really erase $600 billion in market cap?
   - Jensen Huang’s sudden exit from Nvidia sent ripple effects through the entire AI infrastructure market.
   - Explain the risks of relying on a single leader or company for enterprise AI strategy.
   - The broader market impact on AI infrastructure companies like Nebius and CoreWeave.
   - CTA: What is the most important trait for an AI tech leader today?
4. **Claude vs. The Rest: The New Productivity Suite** (Infographic) - Visualizing how AI assistants are moving from simple text chats to integrated workplace tools.
   - Anthropic is changing the game: Claude isn't just a chatbot anymore.
   - New: Claude Meetings (beta) live-transcribes and builds documents in Google Meet.
   - New: Claude Sheets (beta) for collaborative spreadsheet editing.
   - New: Claude Whiteboards (beta) for real-time team planning and deadline tracking.
   - Key differentiator: The tool now works inside your existing workflow tools.
5. **Is Your Business Claimed on ChatGPT?** (Poll) - Highlighting the new Business Hub opportunity for brands to control their identity and visibility within generative search.
   - Poll: Have you claimed your brand's Business Profile on ChatGPT yet?
   - Option: Yes, I've already updated our info.
   - Option: No, I didn't know I could.
   - Option: I don't see the value yet.
   - Option: I'm waiting for more info.
   - Explain that claiming the profile controls how your brand shows up in AI-generated answers.
6. **When the Cloud Goes Dark: A Marketing Wake-up Call** (Thread) - Reflecting on the fragility of digital infrastructure based on the major FAA and Microsoft 365 outages.
   - The November 13th FAA and Microsoft outages were a global wake-up call.
   - 12,000+ flights canceled and massive corporate productivity losses.
   - The core issue: Decades-old infrastructure struggling to support modern cloud dependency.
   - Marketing takeaway: Don't build your entire engagement strategy on a single platform.
   - Always have a manual, offline-capable backup for your communications.
7. **The Future of Tech is... Catching Rockets?** (Short video) - Connecting SpaceX’s 'Mechazilla' achievement to the broader trend of high-stakes, cinematic innovation content.
