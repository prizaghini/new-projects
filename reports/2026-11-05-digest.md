## Content ideas for social media

1. **Stop Optimizing for Taglines, Start Optimizing for Facts** (Carousel) - New research shows that AI recommendation engines ignore marketing fluff in favor of hard homepage data.
   - AI tools like ChatGPT were tested against 704 websites to see what drives a 'recommendation' vs. a 'citation'.
   - Findings prove that taglines and word count have zero impact on whether an AI recommends your brand.
   - To get recommended, you must include specific, checkable statements (homepage facts) about your services.
   - Vague phrases like 'innovative solutions' or 'passionate team' are disregarded as non-facts by models.
   - Brands in the top quarter of Domain Authority already get 44% of recommendations, but the rest must earn them through fact-based copy.
   - Review your homepage today: transform every subjective claim into a verifiable fact for the AI age.
2. **The 'Strawberry' Myth is Dead: AI Just Got Smarter** (Short video) - Recent Stanford data proves that the common jokes about AI being unable to count are officially outdated.
   - Hook: Remember when everyone laughed because AI couldn't count the 'R's in strawberry?
   - Stanford researchers just retested 50 famous AI failures against 20 modern models.
   - The result? Almost half of those famous flaws are completely gone, including the counting error.
   - Models are also much better at finding facts buried in long documents and providing real (not made-up) sources.
   - Conclusion: If you're still using 2023 AI benchmarks to judge your strategy, you're falling behind.
   - CTA: Tell us which AI 'hallucination' you still see most often in the comments!
3. **Is AI Eating Your IT Budget? 2027 Projections** (Poll) - Forrester predicts a massive shift in tech spending, forcing brands to justify high AI costs with real business value.
   - Poll Question: Forrester predicts AI will be 20% of tech spending by 2027. Where is your budget going?
   - Option 1: Heavy AI Investment (15%+)
   - Option 2: Moderate AI Pilot (5-15%)
   - Option 3: Keeping AI Spending Low (<5%)
   - Context: Productivity gains alone won't justify the spend; you need business transformation.
4. **Big Changes to Your Google Ads Workflow** (Thread) - Google is simplifying targeting and testing new CTAs, which could shift your search performance metrics.
   - Google has officially removed language targeting from individual search campaigns to streamline settings.
   - The platform is also testing a prominent 'Visit Site' link directly in search ads.
   - New 'Ask Advisor' features are now appearing in billing reports to help manage spend via AI.
   - These shifts suggest Google is pushing harder toward automated, broad-market efficiency.
   - Check your current search campaigns: how will the removal of specific language filters affect your localized reach?
   - Monitor your CTR closely if the 'Visit Site' CTA rolls out to your account.
5. **Why Anthropic’s AP Deal Changes the AI Race** (Newsletter) - Licensing deals are becoming the new moat for AI companies, starting with real-time election data.
   - Anthropic just signed its first major licensing deal with the Associated Press.
   - This gives the Claude model access to real-time, verified data for critical events like elections.
   - It signals a move away from just 'crawling' the web to secure, authorized data partnerships.
   - For marketers, this means AI answers will become increasingly authoritative and news-current.
   - Watch for more brands needing to prove 'ROI' for their presence within these high-value AI responses.
6. **How Revolut Cut Their AI Bill by 10x** (Infographic) - AI costs are becoming a major enterprise concern, but there are ways to scale without breaking the bank.
   - Case Study: Revolut recently achieved a 10x reduction in their AI operational costs.
   - The Strategy: Moving away from high-priced hosted models toward internal/self-managed solutions.
   - The Context: Scale matters—at scale, tokens can cost $6,000+ per month for enterprise conversations.
   - The Lesson: Audit your 'token' usage now before the 2027 budget hike predicted by Forrester.
   - Alternative: Consider 'weight editing'—researchers just improved a math score by 40% by editing only 0.016% of weights instead of a full retraining run.
