   - Slide 3: The Couch Test Check #1: Check your font size. If a viewer has to lean in from their couch to read on-screen text, the text is too small.

   - Slide 5: The Couch Test Check #3: Audio leveling. Phone speakers compress audio differently than TV soundbars—muddy audio causes instant click-offs.
