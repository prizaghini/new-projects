1. **Why Instagram's Pivot to 90-Minute Video Changes Everything** (Carousel) - Instagram is directly challenging YouTube with long-form video while providing a built-in Reels distribution funnel.
   - Slide 1: Instagram is testing 'Features'—a brand new format supporting video uploads up to 90 minutes long.
   - Slide 2: To solve discovery, their test includes 'Clips as Reels', letting creators cut short snippets that link directly back to the full 90-minute video.
   - Slide 3: This comes as Meta reports user time spent on Instagram reached an all-time high in Q3.
   - Slide 4: However, 45% of recommended Reels are now under 24 hours old, meaning discovery windows for short clips are shrinking rapidly.
   - Slide 5: Action plan: treat Reels as top-of-funnel discovery hooks, and use long-form Features to build deep audience retention.
2. **How People Inc. Replaced Search Traffic With a 625% Social Video Surge** (Thread) - With Google search traffic declining across digital media, publisher revenue strategies are aggressively pivoting toward serialized social video.
   - 1/ Google search traffic now makes up just 20% of total traffic for major publisher People Inc.
   - 2/ Instead of fighting zero-click search pages, they pivoted resources into serialized video shows for People, Travel + Leisure, and Entertainment Weekly.
   - 3/ The payoff: sponsorship revenue across their social franchises exploded by 625% year-over-year.
   - 4/ As Google tests an 'agentic layer' where search and tasks merge into 'one box', expect search referrals to shrink further across all industries.
   - 5/ The takeaway: don't wait for your organic search referrals to evaporate—build native, episodic social video IPs that sponsors can back directly.
