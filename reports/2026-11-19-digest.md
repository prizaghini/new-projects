## Content ideas for social media

1. **Is your homepage ready for AI?** (Thread) - Explain why the homepage is the primary landing zone for AI bots and how to optimize it for machine discovery.
   - AI bots are visiting your homepage 15x more than any other page on your site.
   - On many sites, the homepage gets more bot traffic than all service pages combined.
   - Stop relying on just slogans; AI needs factual data like client names, certifications, and capabilities to properly categorize your brand.
   - Use a 'Brand Manifest' section at the bottom of your page to provide AI agents with a factual, evidence-backed summary of what you do.
   - Balancing human readability with machine-readable facts is the new requirement for SEO.
2. **71% of companies have AI agents. Why aren't they working?** (Carousel) - Highlight the massive gap between experimentation and actual business production.
   - The gap: 71% of companies are using AI agents, but only 11% reach production.
   - The reason: Nearly half of all agents are running in disconnected, siloed environments.
   - Don't start by asking 'build or buy'; start by asking if your workflow can actually integrate these tools.
   - Production is only possible when you move from isolated agents to governed business orchestration.
   - Treat AI agents as scalable business capabilities rather than just cool gadgets.
3. **OpenAI vs. Claude: The cost war heats up** (Short video) - Breaking down the latest price/performance claims from OpenAI's new model release.
   - Hook: OpenAI just dropped a new model that claims to be 20x cheaper than the competition.
   - Point: GPT-6.2 Astra is live now, and they are claiming it beats Claude Fable on professional tasks.
   - Point: OpenAI is marketing this specifically for professional-work tasks, not just chat.
   - Point: It is already available for API, Enterprise, and Edu users.
   - Call to Action: If you're building with AI, have you tested these new models yet?
4. **Search ads are evolving into the AI era** (Infographic) - Summarizing the major shifts in how PPC platforms are handling ads in AI-generated search environments.
   - Google is now live with multimodal asset generation and multiturn ad experiences.
   - Microsoft is taking a more aggressive stance, placing search ads at the very top of AI-generated search results.
   - AI search is no longer just a trend; it's a new placement category for your PPC budget.
   - The goal of these updates is to keep brands visible in non-linear, AI-driven search journeys.
   - Ensure your assets are ready for a multi-turn conversation, not just a single keyword trigger.
5. **The biggest threat to your security isn't just phishing anymore** (Poll) - Addressing the rising concern of AI being used for malicious, automated exploit development.
   - Poll: Are you concerned about AI-driven security exploits?
   - Option 1: Extremely worried.
   - Option 2: Somewhat concerned.
   - Option 3: Not worried yet.
   - Point: Google reports that attackers are now using AI to read security patches and build exploits faster than companies can deploy them.
   - Point: The speed of vulnerability discovery is now faster than manual patch management.
6. **Stop siloing your AI strategy** (Newsletter) - A deep dive into why enterprise AI failure is almost always a process problem, not a technology problem.
   - Most AI agents fail because they are disconnected from end-to-end business processes.
   - The 'build or buy' debate distracts from the real issue: orchestration.
   - If your agent isn't governed by the same risk controls as your human staff, it will never reach production.
   - Start by mapping your current processes and identifying where agents can actually bridge a functional gap.
   - Orchestration is the key to turning an experiment into a scalable business capability.
