## Content ideas for social media

1. **Is your thumbnail failing the 'Hover' test?** (Short video) - Explain the new YouTube behavior of auto-playing videos on hover and provide a fix.
   - Hook: Your YouTube video is playing before people click it.
   - Explain: When users hover over a video, it starts playing, making the first few seconds a critical part of the click decision.
   - The Fix: Use a 'hover hook' strategy—ensure the first frame of your video is a visual match for your thumbnail.
   - CTA: Try matching your first frame to your thumbnail today and check your analytics.
2. **TikTok is splitting in two: Here’s why.** (Thread) - Analyze the strategic implications of TikTok's move to launch a standalone shop app.
   - TikTok has officially launched a standalone shop app in Southeast Asia.
   - The main app remains the 'window' for discovery and traffic.
   - The new app acts as the 'till' to handle the transaction experience.
   - This is a stark contrast to Instagram, which killed its shopping tab in 2023.
   - Prediction: This will likely lead to even more aggressive, shop-centric ad targeting in the main app.
3. **Are your Reels ready for the big screen?** (Carousel) - Discuss the potential for horizontal 'Features' content and why Instagram's TV experiment matters.
   - Instagram is testing an app for TV to show longer 'Features' videos.
   - Why it makes sense: TV is a high-demand medium for longer-form, landscape content.
   - Meta is chasing YouTube's ad revenue, which works well on TV.
   - The opportunity: Longer, more valuable ad placements could finally lead to a better creator revenue-share model.
   - Final thought: Is your video library formatted for landscape or ready to adapt?
4. **Retail Media: Everyone is getting into the game** (Infographic) - Visualize the explosion of retail media networks (RMNs) using the recent RaceTrac announcement.
   - New entrant: RaceTrac just launched Trac Media Group.
   - The strategy: Converting 6 million loyalty members into ad-targeted audiences.
   - Context: The c-store/fuel retail media space is getting crowded.
   - Competitors: 7-Eleven, Wawa, Love’s, Circle K, and Sam’s Club are all doing the same.
   - Takeaway: If you have a loyalty program and physical inventory, you are now an ad platform.
5. **Does your content menu need a refresh?** (Poll) - Engage followers by letting them decide which type of content provides the most value.
   - Question: Which type of content is currently driving the most growth for your brand?
   - Behind-the-scenes teasers (Starters)
   - Deep-dive educational content (Expert Choice)
   - High-production product showcases (Service Specials)
   - None of the above—I'm still experimenting!
6. **3 Rules for better YouTube Retention** (Thread) - Translate the concept of the retention graph into an actionable content strategy.
   - Stop seeing your retention graph as a report card—view it as a map.
   - Sharp drop-off in the first 30 seconds? Your hook didn't deliver on the promise of your thumbnail.
   - Gradual decline? You're taking too long to provide value.
   - Action: Identify the specific pattern in your latest video and shift the structure for the next one.
   - Remember: Even a single video change can lead to massive growth milestones (like 1M subscribers).
