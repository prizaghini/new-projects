## Content ideas for social media

1. **Why your marketing resume is getting ignored (Hint: It's AI)** (Thread) - With job requirements shifting fast, highlighting AI skills is now an absolute must for modern marketers.
   - Marketing roles requiring AI literacy jumped by 113% recently, according to new LinkedIn data.
   - Shockingly, only 4% of marketers actually list AI skills on their profiles right now.
   - Candidates who do include AI literacy on their LinkedIn profiles are 78% more likely to get hired.
   - Don't get filtered out—update your profile to showcase how you are actively using AI in your day-to-day work.
2. **Stop filming 90-minute videos from scratch for Instagram** (Carousel) - Instagram's new Features and Clips test means long-form repurposing is finally getting easier.
   - Instagram is testing 'Features' allowing select creators to upload horizontal videos up to 90 minutes long.
   - You don't need to create feature-length content from scratch if you already record podcasts or webinars.
   - Use 'Clips as Reels' to slice up longer videos into bite-sized snippets.
   - Every clip can directly link back to your full-length video, driving continuous traffic.
3. **YouTube Shorts just changed the rules on reposted content** (Short video) - Originality is winning on YouTube Shorts right now—here is what creators need to adjust.
   - Hook: YouTube Shorts is cracking down on simple re-uploads. Here is how to keep your reach safe.
   - Point 1: YouTube is updating recommendation systems to give genuine original content much higher visibility.
   - Point 2: Repurposing content is still allowed, but mindless re-uploading without added value will hurt your reach.
   - Point 3: Inject your own perspective by adding commentary, personal expertise, or storytelling.
   - CTA: Stop chasing every viral trend and start sharing content only your brand can provide. Drop a comment with your take!
4. **Reddit just made a massive change to your feed** (Poll) - Reddit's new For You feed gives users a brand-new way to consume content—how will you adapt?
   - Poll Question: How do you plan to use Reddit's new 'For You' feed vs. 'Following' view?
   - Option A: I will rely heavily on the algorithm-driven 'For You' feed.
   - Option B: I prefer sticking strictly to the 'Following' tab for curated content.
   - Option C: I use both depending on what I am searching for.
5. **The new rules for Instagram Carousels in 2026** (Infographic) - Adam Mosseri just revealed multiple new ways carousels get extra distribution.
   - Slide 1: Add music to your carousels to make them fully eligible for the Reels feed.
   - Slide 2: Carousels now get a second chance in the feed if a user scrolls past them initially.
   - Slide 3: On the second pass, the carousel reappears starting automatically with the second slide.
   - Slide 4: Multiple slides give users more distinct opportunities to like, comment, and share.
6. **Big change for Google PMAX advertisers: A/B testing is here** (Other) - Google Ads just rolled out a cleaner way to test asset group adjustments without guessing.
   - Google Performance Max (PMAX) now allows advertisers to save asset group edits directly as an A/B test.
   - This update removes guesswork when testing creative variations within your asset groups.
   - PPC marketers can track performance changes with cleaner test structures.
   - Make sure to test one distinct asset group variation at a time to maximize statistical significance.
