# LinkedIn caption: Business Card post

LinkedIn just gave everyone a digital business card, and it's free.

So, back in the middle of October, LinkedIn announced they were going to roll it out to everyone over the next few months. And I've just found out mine is here! The best part? You don't need a Premium subscription to get one, so thank you LinkedIn. Honestly, it's fantastic and I'm so excited about it.

Before this, all we had was the trusty little profile QR code from 2018. Bless it. You scanned it, it took you to someone's profile, and that was pretty much its whole personality.

This one has had a proper glow-up. It finally feels like 2026:

- You pick the layout, font, colours and pattern
- You can pop it in your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, so it's right there when you're at an event
- You can scan other people's cards and keep them all in one place
- And it updates whenever your profile does, so no more reprinting cards every time you change jobs

Honestly, the bit I love most is how quick it is. Scan, connect, move on. No typing names into search, and no more "is that Priscila with one L or two?"

So of course I had to make mine: black, grid, bold type, and my Treze Media logo on the back. Swipe to have a look.

Have you got yours yet, or are you still waiting for it to pop up? Let me know in the comments.
