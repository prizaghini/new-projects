# LinkedIn caption: Business Card post

LinkedIn is rolling out a free digital business card to everyone, and mine has just arrived!

They first announced this back in mid-October, and the best part is you don't need a Premium subscription to get one. Thank you, LinkedIn.

Before this, all we had was the trusty little profile QR code from 2018. Bless it. You scanned it, it took you to someone's profile, and that was pretty much its whole personality.

Well, it's finally had a proper glow-up, and it actually feels like 2026. Now you can:

• Pick your own layout, font, colours and pattern
• Pop it in your Apple or Google Wallet for easy access at events
• Scan other people's cards to add them to your collection
• Change jobs without reprinting a thing, as it updates automatically with your profile

Of course, I couldn't wait to design mine: black, grid, bold type, and my Treze Media logo on the back. Swipe to have a look.

Honestly, the bit I love most is how quick it is. Scan, connect, move on. No typing names into search, and no more "is that Priscila with a double L?"

(For the record: Priscila, single L. Cilla, double. If you know, you know.)

Have you got yours yet, or are you still waiting for it to pop up?
