# LinkedIn caption: Business Card post

LinkedIn is giving everyone a digital business card, and it's free.

So, back in the middle of October, LinkedIn announced they were going to roll it out to everyone over the next few months. And I've just found out mine is here! The best part? You don't need a Premium subscription to get one, so thank you LinkedIn.

Before this, all we had was the trusty little profile QR code from 2018. Bless it. You scanned it, it took you to someone's profile, and that was pretty much its whole personality.

Well, it's finally had a proper glow-up, and it actually feels like 2026. Now you can:

- pick your own layout, font, colours and pattern
- pop it in your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, so it's right there when you're at an event
- scan other people's cards to add them to your collection
- change jobs without reprinting a thing, because it updates whenever your profile does

So as soon as mine landed, I made it my own: black, grid, bold type, and my Treze Media logo on the back. Swipe to have a look.

Honestly, the bit I love most is how quick it is. Scan, connect, move on. No typing names into search, and no more "is that Priscila with double L?" (For the record: Priscila, single L. Cilla, double. If you know, you know.)

Have you got yours yet, or are you still waiting for it to pop up?
