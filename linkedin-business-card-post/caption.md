# LinkedIn caption: Business Card post

LinkedIn just gave everyone a digital business card, and it's free. 🪪

So, back in the middle of October, LinkedIn announced they were going to roll it out to everyone over the next few months. And no, you don't need a Premium subscription for it.

Do you remember the old profile QR code from 2018? You scanned it, it took you to someone's profile, and that was pretty much it.

This one actually feels like yours:

🎨 You pick the layout, font, colours and pattern
📲 You can pop it in your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, so it's right there when you're at an event
📇 You can scan other people's cards and keep them all in one place
🔄 And it updates whenever your profile does, so no more reprinting cards every time you change jobs

Honestly, the bit I love most is how quick it is. Scan, connect, move on. No typing names into search, no "is that one L or two?" 😅

So of course I had to make mine: black, grid, bold type, and my 13 on the back. Swipe to have a look 👉
